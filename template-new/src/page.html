The model

You sit down with the artist who will do the work. You see the drawing before you pay anything beyond the deposit. If it isn't right, we redraw it — that part has never been billable.

The record

Roughly 2,400 pieces finished, 4.9 across 380 reviews, and single-use needles opened in front of you every time.

The things
everyone asks first.

How much is this going to cost me, really?

You get a written number at the consult, and it only moves if you change the design. If the work runs over, we eat the difference.

Does it hurt?

Yes, but less than you're picturing. We break whenever you need one — nobody has ever been talked out of a break here.

What if I hate the drawing?

Then we redraw it, and that has never been billable. Nothing goes on your skin until you've said yes out loud.

Is the deposit refundable?

It comes off your final total, so you never pay extra for it. Move with 48 hours' notice and it follows you.

Come in,
talk it through,
pay nothing.
