The studio

We turned down the walk-in trade in 2016, and the work got twice as good.

Blackthorn is four artists, four chairs, and a booking sheet that stops at what we can actually finish well. Thirteen years, roughly 2,400 pieces, and a apprenticeship program that has put three artists into their own shops.

The shop started in a room above a bike repair place on Fifth. One chair, one machine, and a hand-painted sign that took longer than the first month of rent. What changed everything was the day we stopped taking walk-ins: fewer tattoos, but every one of them drawn properly first.

That's still the whole model. You sit down with the artist who will do the work. You see the drawing before you pay anything beyond the deposit. And if it isn't right, we redraw it — that part has never been billable.

Rae Okonjo Owner · Resident artist since 2012

If that's the way you'd want yours done, come and sit down with us — the consult is free, and it's the easiest way to find out.

Straight answers

The things everyone asks first.

How much is this going to cost me, really?

You get a written number at the consult, and it only moves if you change the design. If we misjudge and the work runs over, we eat the difference — that's our problem, not your bill.

Does it hurt?

Yes, but less than you're picturing, and it's very manageable in the spots most people start with. We break whenever you need one — nobody has ever been talked out of a break here.

What if I hate the drawing?

Then we redraw it, and that has never been billable. Nothing goes on your skin until you've seen the stencil placed and said yes out loud.

I don't know exactly what I want yet.

That's the normal starting point, not a problem. Bring references, a rough idea, or just the story — pulling a design out of that conversation is the part we're actually good at.

Is the deposit refundable?

It comes straight off your final total, so you're never paying extra for it. Move your appointment with 48 hours' notice and it follows you; below that it covers the artist's held day.

How do I know it's clean in there?

Single-use needles and tubes, opened in front of you, every time. The shop is licensed and inspected annually, and the certificate is on the wall by the register — ask to see anything you want.
